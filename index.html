Our Story

The Saffron Table opened in 2015 as a small family kitchen with one big idea: good food brings people together. Today, we are a neighbourhood favourite known for our friendly service and warm, relaxed atmosphere.

Our speciality is modern comfort food with an Indian heart: rich slow-cooked curries, hand-rolled pastas and desserts baked fresh every morning.
